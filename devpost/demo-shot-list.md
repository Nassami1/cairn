# Demo shot list

> Recording mechanics and timings only. The narration is the entrant's own words — write it before
> recording, or speak it live. The goal is a viewer who has never heard of Cairn understanding the
> problem, seeing the working kernel, and knowing how to try it, in under three minutes.

## Setup

- `npm run build && npm start`, open <http://localhost:8787>. Start with a **fresh server** so the first
  claim runs live rather than from the 30-minute cache.
- 1440×900 browser window, 100% zoom, bookmarks bar hidden, no other tabs or keys on screen.
- Close notifications. Use one claim as the centrepiece; the Great Wall claim has the strongest quotes.
- Have the repository URL ready for the final frame.

## Shots

| Time | What is on screen | What the viewer should understand |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | The empty desk. Scroll slowly from the headline to the composer. | The problem: a confident answer is not the same as a checked one. |
| 0:20–0:35 | Stay on the desk; point at the three source families and the confidence note in the field-notes column. | Who it is for: someone who has to defend a claim, not just repeat it. |
| 0:35–0:55 | Click the Great Wall example. Do not narrate over the first two seconds; let the stage rail move. | The work is real and streaming: understand, retrieve, read, compare, write. |
| 0:55–1:20 | Source cards arrive. Hover the stance tags and let the first contradiction be readable. | Sources are read, not counted; each card carries a quote and a stance. |
| 1:20–1:45 | Land on the verdict: Contradicted, the headline, then the answer with citation markers. | The output is a verdict with its argument attached. |
| 1:45–2:05 | Click `[1]` (or the strongest counter-source). The card scrolls, expands, shows the passage and the outbound link. | Every claim in the answer can be traced to a real page. |
| 2:05–2:30 | Scroll to the confidence cairn and the four factors; read one factor note out loud. | Confidence is derived, capped, and inspectable — not a decorative percentage. |
| 2:30–2:45 | Click **Copy the receipt**; show the toast, then optionally the pasted text. | The result is usable in the entrant's real workflow. |
| 2:45–3:00 | Cut to the repository page (license visible) and the run command in the README. | A judge can clone and run it without a key. |

Keep it under 3:00. If a provider fails during the recording, keep the run: the run notes and the lower
confidence are the product's honesty, and they are worth a sentence.

## Checklist before upload

- [ ] The verdict and a quoted source are visible on screen.
- [ ] The cursor shows the citation click and the expansion.
- [ ] The confidence factors are legible at 1080p.
- [ ] No keys, email addresses, or unrelated windows appear.
- [ ] Audio is the entrant's own voice; no licensed music.
- [ ] Uploaded to YouTube or Vimeo as public or unlisted-with-link, link tested in a private window.
